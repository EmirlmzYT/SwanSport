# Wave 1 Plan: Core Shell, Global Scroll Fluidity, Shimmer Skeletons, Feed & Primary Tabs

<baseline>
- Target Application: apps/swansport_app (mobil + web). Konsola (apps/swansport_console) ve swansport_design_system paketine degisiklik YASAKTIR.
- Hedef: Codex Writer'in uygulamayi donen cemberlerden (CircularProgressIndicator) arindirip, global elastik (bouncing) kaydirma, zero-layout-shift shimmer iskeletler ve Instagram standardinda premium Feed, Kesfet, Mesajlar, Profil akiciligina kavusturmasi.
</baseline>

---

## Task 1: Global Scroll Fluidity (swansport_app.dart)

Global elastik (bouncing) kaydirma fizigini sozlesmesine bagla:
1. apps/swansport_app/lib/app/swansport_app.dart dosyasini ac.
2. MaterialApp icinde scrollBehavior ozelligini tanimla:
`dart
scrollBehavior: const MaterialScrollBehavior().copyWith(
  physics: const BouncingScrollPhysics(
    parent: AlwaysScrollableScrollPhysics(),
  ),
),
`
3. Boylece tum ListView, CustomScrollView ve SingleChildScrollView'lar varsayilan olarak elastik kaydirma hissiyati kazanir.

---

## Task 2: Shimmer Skeleton Altyapisi (swan_skeleton.dart & stitch_components.dart)

Cember donmesi yerine zero-layout-shift saglayan guclu iskeletler bilesene eklenmelidir:
1. apps/swansport_app/lib/app/widgets/swan_skeleton.dart icine:
   - SwanCardSkeleton bileseni ekle (ustte 40x40 avatar shimmer + baslik, ortada 180px gorsel/icerik blogu, altta 2 satir shimmer line).
   - SwanGridSkeleton bileseni ekle (2 sutunlu saha/galeri icin).
   - premiumLoading() ve premiumCardLoading() fonksiyonlarini bagla.
2. stitch_components.dart icindeki premiumLoading() fonksiyonunun sayfa yuksekligini dolduran tutarli SwanListSkeleton dondurdugunden emin ol.

---

## Task 3: Feed Ekrani & PostCard Akiciligi (feed_screen.dart, post_card.dart)

1. apps/swansport_app/lib/features/social/presentation/feed_screen.dart:
   - _FeedModeBar pinned filtre seridinin aktif durum gecislerini yumusak AnimatedContainer veya StitchFilterPill ile bagla.
   - loading dalinda: Center(child: CircularProgressIndicator()) yerine premiumLoading() veya SwanCardSkeleton kullan.
   - Sayfa kenar padding: 16px (SwanSpace.lg).
2. apps/swansport_app/lib/features/social/presentation/widgets/post_card.dart:
   - Material + InkWell dokunma hissiyati (borderRadius: BorderRadius.circular(SwanRadius.md)).
   - Kart kenarligi: border: Border.all(color: c.line.withValues(alpha: isDark ? 0.35 : 0.6), width: 0.8).
   - Kalp (like) butonuna tiklandiginda akici dokunma geri bildirimi.
   - kTeal, dangerRed ham renklerini temizle, context.swan jetonlarina gotur.

---

## Task 4: Kesfet Ekrani Modernizasyonu (explore_screen.dart)

1. apps/swansport_app/lib/features/network/presentation/explore_screen.dart:
   - StitchSearchBar dokunma hissiyati: Arama cubuguna tiklandiginda /arama rotasina akici gecis.
   - Yatay brans haplari (StitchFilterPill): BouncingScrollPhysics ile kaydirma.
   - LinearProgressIndicator ve CircularProgressIndicator kaldirilmasi: Yerine yatay SwanShimmer iskeletleri.
   - Saha ve topluluk bloklarinda 14px (SwanRadius.md) yuzey kartlari ve puruzsuz InkWell tepkisi.

## Task 5: Mesajlar, Profil ve Arama Ekranlari (messages_screen.dart, profile_screen.dart, search_screen.dart)

1. apps/swansport_app/lib/features/social/presentation/messages_screen.dart:
   - SwanSegmentedTabs ile Direkt ve Topluluklar sekmeleri arasinda akici gecis.
   - Sohbet listesinde online gostergesi, unread badge ve Material + InkWell dokunma etkisi.
   - Yukleme aninda CircularProgressIndicator yerine sohbet satir iskeleti (SwanListSkeleton).
2. apps/swansport_app/lib/features/social/presentation/profile_screen.dart:
   - Profil basligi istatistik bloklarinda 14px (SwanRadius.md), zarif batimli box-shadow.
   - Sekmeler puruzsuz SwanSegmentedTabs ile hizalanmisken, liste ve grid gecisleri elastik bouncing scroll'a dayandirilsin.
3. apps/swansport_app/lib/features/social/presentation/search_screen.dart:
   - Arama giris alaninda aninda dokunma geri bildirimi ve sonuc dilinde SwanListSkeleton.
